# AI Engineering Service

## Overview

AI Engineering focuses on "connecting systems, automating workflows, and multiplying impact" by
integrating disconnected tools and adding intelligence to repetitive processes.

## What They Build

**Workflow automations:**
- Automating data entry, document routing, email triage, report generation, and customer
  inquiry handling.
- Connecting APIs across a client's tech stack.
- Building flows that sync data in real time, trigger actions across platforms, and eliminate
  manual copy-paste work.

**Custom AI agents:**
- Autonomous systems for complex sales research, multi-step customer service, or operations that
  run independently.
- Flows that understand context, route based on content analysis, and extract information from
  unstructured data.

## LLM Selection & Data Security Approach

This is Cadre AI's stated approach to LLM selection and data security:

- They help select the LLM that fits a client's specific use cases — not a one-size-fits-all
  model choice.
- They emphasize keeping client data in a "black box" so it's never used to train other models.
- A key risk they call out explicitly: employees sharing company secrets in personal/consumer
  LLM accounts without oversight. Their engagements are partly about preventing that.
- They implement AI tooling that is secure and compliant for the whole organization, rather than
  leaving AI adoption to ungoverned individual tool use.
- They work across multiple platforms: Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Mistral, DeepSeek, and
  others — selecting and configuring per client rather than pushing a single vendor.

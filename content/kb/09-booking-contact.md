# Booking a Call & Contact Information

## How to Book a Call

The primary call-to-action across the Cadre AI site is **"Talk to an AI Strategist"**, which
links to `/contact`. There's no self-serve scheduling link described beyond this — the contact
form is the entry point.

## Getting an AI Maturity Index

Also requested via `/contact` — "Get Your AI Maturity Index." See `07-ai-maturity-index.md` for
what it evaluates.

## Pricing

There is no public pricing on cadreai.com. Pricing is scoped per engagement after a
conversation with a strategist — the bot should never invent a number and should always route
pricing questions to `/contact`.

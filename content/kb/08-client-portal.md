# Client Portal

Cadre AI offers clients a centralized portal to track their AI tools, agents, training, and
results — built around alignment and accountability as an engagement progresses.

This chatbot does not have access to any individual client's portal data or account status. If
someone asks about the status of their specific engagement, tools, or results, that requires
looking them up directly — the bot should escalate rather than guess, and point them to their
Cadre AI contact or `/contact` for account-specific questions.

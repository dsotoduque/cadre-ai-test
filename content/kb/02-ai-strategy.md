# AI Strategy Service

## What It Includes

The AI Strategy service is a structured 45-day program called the **AI Transformation
Intensive**, taking a business from lack of clarity to a prioritized, executable roadmap. Cadre
AI emphasizes tangible results over theory: "we don't do massive presentations and walk away. We
find quick wins that create measurable EBITDA impact."

## Methodology: Four Phases

1. **Discover use cases** — interview teams, calculate potential ROI, and prioritize by impact
   and feasibility.
2. **Research the landscape** — evaluate existing tools and emerging capabilities.
3. **Implement solutions** — deploy and train teams.
4. **Scale with confidence** — expand to other departments and build a 3-year roadmap.

## The AI Maturity Index

As part of AI Strategy work, Cadre AI offers the **AI Maturity Index**, an assessment that scores
a company across an eight-pillar framework for AI transformation. Full detail on the eight
pillars is in `07-ai-maturity-index.md`.

## How to Get Started

Engage via **"Talk to an AI Strategist"**, available on the site and at `/contact`. The AI
Maturity Index itself is also requested via `/contact` ("Get Your AI Maturity Index").

# AI Agents Service

## What Are Cadre AI's Agents

Cadre AI builds "autonomous workers that handle your most complex tasks," offered at three
levels of implementation:

- **Prompts & Assistants** — simple tools that automate repetitive tasks.
- **Voice Agents** — agents that handle phone conversations for data capture.
- **Fully Fledged AI Agents** — complex systems that plan, execute actions across multiple
  tools, and manage entire processes.

## Example Agent Library (18+ specialized agents)

**Operations:**
- Budget Request Reviewer — reviews budget requests against policy.
- Resource Scheduler — optimizes resource allocation across projects.
- Change Order Tracker — automatically documents scope changes.

**Sales & Marketing:**
- B2B Sales Automator — manages outreach and sales follow-up.
- LinkedIn Legend — generates consistent professional content.
- Website Chatbot — answers customer questions 24/7 (this is the kind of agent this very
  chatbot is an example of).

**Finance:**
- Client Risk Assessor — automatically evaluates risk profiles.
- Market Alert Monitor — monitors real estate market trends.
- KYC Automation Accelerator — speeds up client verification.

## Key Benefit

Agents are designed to drive revenue, improve profitability, and elevate what employees can
focus on — taking repetitive, complex work off their plate.

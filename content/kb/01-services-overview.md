# What Cadre AI Does

## Mission

Cadre AI helps businesses turn enterprise AI into real profitability, scale, and business value.
They work with companies to drive revenue through AI-powered automation and to optimize
operations to increase profit margins — moving businesses from AI confusion to AI confidence.

## Core Services

Cadre AI offers four core services:

1. **AI Strategy** — Discover how AI improves the business; identify high-ROI opportunities
   through a structured program (see `02-ai-strategy.md`).
2. **AI Leadership & Facilitation** — Turn teams into AI champions through experiential sessions,
   not theoretical lectures (see `03-ai-leadership-facilitation.md`).
3. **AI Engineering** — Orchestrate intelligent processes, connect systems, and automate
   workflows (see `04-ai-engineering.md`).
4. **AI Agents** — Autonomous workers that handle a business's most complex tasks (see
   `05-ai-agents.md`).

## How They Work With Clients

Cadre AI acts as an integrated AI team — strategists, leadership facilitators, and engineers —
rather than handing over a slide deck and leaving. They report over 100 high-ROI AI use cases
implemented across 50+ companies, and cite a 300% higher success rate when working with a
specialized partner versus building AI initiatives purely in-house.

## Company Values (Four Pillars)

Cadre AI's team operates on four core values:

1. **Growth mindset** — "lifelong learners on a mission to improve 1% every day."
2. **Extreme ownership** — full responsibility for both the problem and the solution.
3. **Teamwork** — "we win together, we lose together."
4. **Agility** — adaptability, resourcefulness, speed.

## Leadership

Cadre AI is led by CEO Grayson Lafrenz, with co-founders Riley Stricklin and Chad Lohrli in key
strategic roles.

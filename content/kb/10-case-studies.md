# Case Studies

Client company names are not publicly disclosed on cadreai.com's case studies page — only

## Professional Services — Lead Processing Agent

A sales coordinator was manually monitoring a shared inbox, spending 3-5 minutes per lead on CRM
data entry. Cadre built an AI agent that monitors the inbox 24/7, classifies emails, looks up
existing clients in the CRM, creates records automatically, and distributes tasks based on
real-time availability. Result: ~45 hours saved monthly, 1,500+ emails processed and 650+ leads
handled monthly. ("I was thrilled by how fast Cadre delivered. My goal is to free an employee
up, he's capable of so much more." — Zac Davis, Sales Manager)

## Manufacturing & Logistics — Supplier Automation

A team processed 200-300 supplier confirmation emails daily, needing 2-6 minutes per ticket for
manual data extraction and verification. Cadre built an automation that extracts supplier data,
cross-checks it against NetSuite records, and confirms orders automatically, flagging
exceptions. Result: ~220 hours saved monthly, 60% reduction in processing time. ("This can save
us over 1,500 hours every year that now gets spent on more impactful work for our business." —
Bridget Hirsch, Manager)

## Manufacturing & Logistics — Proposal Automation

## Hospitality — AI-Powered Housing Visibility System

An outdated reservation system caused same-day overbookings, costing ~$35,000/month in
expedited cleaning. Cadre built a CRM-integrated visibility dashboard that prevents conflicting
bookings by automatically blocking same-day slots. Result: ~$420,000 saved annually, elimination
of "flip day" incidents.

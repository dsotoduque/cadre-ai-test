# AI Leadership & Facilitation Service

## Purpose

Cadre AI transforms executives and teams through experiential sessions, not theoretical
lectures. The goal is turning "AI-hesitant" professionals into "AI-ambitious" ones, using
facilitation backed by behavioral science and real implementation experience.

## Methodology: A Three-Part Approach

- **30% Teaching** — sharing patterns from real implementations across industries: what worked,
  where others failed, and how to spot AI-ready use cases.
- **30% Interaction** — live problem-solving sessions. Leaders practice as "strategic champions"
  facing real scenarios: skeptical directors, employees afraid of job loss, and competition for
  resources.
- **40% Application** — direct work on concrete business challenges. Teams identify 3-5 specific
  opportunities where AI would create impact, with immediate, prioritized action plans.

## Available Formats

- 2-day leadership intensive
- 1-day workshops
- Half-day executive sessions
- 1-hour video calls

## Expected Outcomes

- Identify viable use cases
- Manage fears and uncertainty productively
- Align leadership and teams
- Leave with actionable plans

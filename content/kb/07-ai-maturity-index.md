# The AI Maturity Index

## What It Is

The AI Maturity Index scores a company across Cadre AI's eight-pillar framework for AI
transformation. It gives a grade in each area with clear explanations, plus actionable insights
on how to improve.

## The Eight Pillars

1. **Dedicated AI team** — a structure with a responsible leader, product experts, strategy,
   research, and engineering roles, under CEO-level sponsorship.
2. **AI Command Center** — selecting a single platform (e.g. ChatGPT, Copilot, Claude) for the
   whole company, typically around $30/month per user.
3. **AI-first cultural change** — clear policies, leadership communication, and change
   management.
4. **AI-enabled tech stack** — API access and an audit of existing AI-capable features.
5. **AI-healthy data** — clean, structured data across the organization.
6. **AI-agent readiness** — mapping processes, infrastructure, and monitoring for digital
   agents.
7. **Department-by-department deep dives** — evaluating each team to identify AI opportunities.
8. **3-year AI vision** — defining the company's ideal, most scalable future state.

## How to Get Scored

The AI Maturity Index is requested via `/contact` — "Get Your AI Maturity Index." It's typically
the entry point into the broader AI Strategy engagement (see `02-ai-strategy.md`).

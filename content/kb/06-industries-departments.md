# Industries and Departments Cadre AI Serves

## Industries Served

Cadre AI works with B2B companies across:

- Professional Services
- Private Equity
- Real Estate
- Financial Services
- Mortgage & Lending
- Construction
- Retail & E-commerce
- Manufacturing & Logistics
- Hospitality

If a prospective client's industry isn't an exact match to this list, Cadre AI still likely
serves adjacent B2B sectors — the bot should say the industries it's confirmed to work in and
offer to connect the person with a strategist to confirm fit for anything more specific, rather
than flatly saying no.

## Departments Covered

Within a client organization, Cadre AI's work spans:

- Sales
- Marketing
- Customer Success
- Executive Leadership
- Finance
- Operations
- Technology
- Legal
